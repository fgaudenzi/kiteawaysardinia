## Everything You Need, Right at the Spot

At Kite Away, we have created a large and comfortable base designed to make your day on the water simple, safe and enjoyable.

### A Spot Built Around Kiters

Our spot is one of the largest in the area, with a **dedicated kite area exclusively for our school and riders**. There are no other kite schools operating around us, giving you plenty of space and a more relaxed environment to launch, land and ride.

Our facilities include:

* **Private parking**
* **Changing room** to get changed comfortably before or after your session
* **Outdoor shower**
* **Wetsuit and gear drying area**
* **Shaded chill-out area**
* **Dedicated area for safe kite launching and landing**
* **Large grass area for comfortably rigging and packing your kite**
* **Electricity to charge your devices or dry your hair after your session**

Everything is designed around one goal: **less time dealing with logistics, more time enjoying the wind.**

### Between Calasetta and Sant'Antioco

Our location is one of our biggest advantages. We are positioned **between Calasetta and Sant'Antioco**, with some of the area's most beautiful beaches just **5 minutes away by car**.

This means that before or after your lesson, you can easily take a break and enjoy a beautiful Sardinian beach, go for a swim or simply relax by the sea.

And if you're looking for even more riding, **Punta Trettu is just one tack away**, making our location an ideal base for exploring the area's kite spots.

### Quality Equipment & Professional Instructors

Our school uses **Ventum Kiteboarding equipment**, selected to provide reliable and high-performance gear for both learning and progression.

All our instructors are **IKO certified**, following internationally recognised teaching and safety standards.

Whether you are taking your first steps into kitesurfing or looking to improve your riding, you will be learning with professional equipment and qualified instructors in a spot designed specifically around the needs of kiters.
